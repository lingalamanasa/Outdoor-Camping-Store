Our Heritage Journey

15 Years of Wild Innovation

From a garage in Boulder, Colorado to outfitting expeditions on 7 continents.

2009 • Genesis

Hand-Stitched Tents in Boulder

Founded by two alpine mountaineers dissatisfied with flimsy commercial tents during a winter storm on Longs Peak.

2014 • Eco-Shift

100% Recycled Technical Fabrics

Eliminated PFC coatings across all shell jackets and switched our entire sleeping bag fill to certified cruelty-free down.

2019 • Technology

Global GPX & Guide Network

Launched our offline mapping system and certified alpine guide network, supporting over 20,000 yearly wilderness trail days.

2026 • Future
