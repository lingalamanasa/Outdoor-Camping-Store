Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is shipping on gear orders?

We offer free tracked 2-day shipping on all orders over $75 within the USA and Canada. Express overnight shipping is available at checkout.

What is your 60-day return & exchange policy?

If your gear doesn't fit or perform as expected, return it unused within 60 days for a 100% full refund or free size exchange. Return shipping labels are pre-paid.

How does the Stackly Lifetime Warranty work?

Every tent, pack, and sleeping bag is warrantied against manufacturing defects for life. If a seam tears or a zipper breaks during normal trail use, send it to our repair clinic for free repair.

Can I rent gear before committing to buy?

Yes! We offer 3-day and 7-day rental packages for tents, backpacks, and stoves. If you decide to buy the item after your trip, 100% of your rental fee is applied toward the purchase price.
