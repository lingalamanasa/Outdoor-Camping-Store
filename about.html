Section 02

Our Journey Through The Years

From a small Boulder workshop to a global leader in outdoor gear.

2009

Founded in Boulder, Colorado

Started by two mountaineers sewing waterproof packs for local Rocky Mountain guides.

2014

First Eco-Certified Tent Series

Launched the AlpineShield tent line made entirely from recycled nylon canvas.

2019

Expanded to 50+ Countries

Opened regional base camp centers in Vancouver, Chamonix, and Queenstown.

2026

Circular Economy Leader

Reached 50,000+ active members with 100% zero-waste packaging & gear recycling program.
